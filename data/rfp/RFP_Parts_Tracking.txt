Request for Proposal - Parts and Supplier Tracking System

=== Page 1 ===
Scope
Maruti requires a system to track spare parts inventory and supplier performance across plants.

Technical requirements
The solution must integrate with existing ERP systems, encrypt data, and keep all data in India.

=== Page 2 ===
Timeline
The system must be fully implemented and live within 6 months of contract signature.

Evaluation
Proposals are evaluated on technical merit. Commercial terms are assessed separately.
