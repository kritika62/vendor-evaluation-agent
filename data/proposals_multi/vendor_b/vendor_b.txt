Beta Soft - Technical Proposal

=== Page 1 ===
Functional coverage
The product covers all mandatory functional requirements and several optional ones, including a built-in supplier scorecard and multi-plant inventory views.

Architecture
A modular monolith deployed on virtual machines. It scales vertically and supports a second node for load sharing.

Integration
Standard REST APIs are available for most modules. Integration with SAP is supported through a certified partner connector.

=== Page 2 ===
Security
We follow ISO 27001 aligned practices and are in the process of certification. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with role-based access control.

Data privacy
Customer data is hosted in a Mumbai cloud region. Data handling follows applicable privacy regulations.

Reliability
A 99.5% uptime target with nightly backups and a standby environment. Recovery time objective is 12 hours.

=== Page 3 ===
Implementation
A 9-month implementation with weekly status reporting. The team will be confirmed after contract signature.

Support
Business-hours support with email and phone escalation, and online training material.

Customisation and roadmap
Configuration is possible through settings. Custom features can be developed under a change request.
