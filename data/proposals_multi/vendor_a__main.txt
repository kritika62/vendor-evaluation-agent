Alpha Systems - Technical Proposal

=== Page 1 ===
Functional coverage
Our platform meets all 20 must-have functional requirements listed in the RFP, including parts tracking, supplier portals and reporting. Section 3 maps each requirement to a product module with live demo references.

Architecture
The solution uses a microservices architecture on Kubernetes with horizontal autoscaling. Load tests at 5,000 concurrent users showed stable response times under 300 ms.

Integration
We expose a documented REST API and provide pre-built connectors for SAP and Oracle ERP, as well as standard EDI and CSV data exchange.

=== Page 2 ===
Security
We hold ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certifications, audited annually. Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3, with role-based access control and full audit logging.

Data privacy
All customer data is stored in Indian data centres, with contractual residency guarantees. We comply with the DPDP Act and offer data deletion on request.

Reliability
We commit to 99.9% uptime with a disaster recovery site in a separate region, an RTO of 4 hours and an RPO of 15 minutes, tested twice a year.

=== Page 3 ===
Implementation
Implementation is planned in four phases over 16 weeks with named milestones, a dedicated delivery team and a risk register. We delivered similar projects for two large automotive manufacturers.

Support
Support is available 24x7 with a 1-hour response time for critical issues, plus onsite training for administrators and quarterly product updates.

Customisation and roadmap
The platform is highly configurable through low-code workflows, and our published roadmap commits to supplier analytics and AI-based forecasting next year.
